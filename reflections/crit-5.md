## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I don't love saying this, since it sounds the same every week, but there
wasn't a clear breakthrough on this one — the build went smoothly from start
to finish.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

This was the first time a rule I'd written in my own `CLAUDE.md` actually
worked against me, even though the real-world impact was tiny (a lowercase
word on a pause screen). It made me realize that when writing a harness going
forward, I need rules that are specific and conditional rather than broad —
a rule that's too general ends up making the model more rigid than helpful.
